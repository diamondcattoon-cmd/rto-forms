FAQ

RC renewal — common questions

Which form is used to renew a vehicle's Registration Certificate?

Form 25, under Rule 52 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 — used to renew the RC of a non-transport vehicle. Fill it online here in the official 2-page format and download a print-ready PDF.

When should I renew my vehicle's RC?

Apply before your current RC's validity expires — submit Form 25 along with the prescribed fee at your registering authority. The expiry date is printed on your existing RC.

Is this the official government form?

Yes — the layout follows the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 format exactly, the same one used nationwide. This is an independent tool, not a government website; always verify with your RTO/DTO before submission.
