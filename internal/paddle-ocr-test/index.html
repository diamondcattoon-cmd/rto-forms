What this page sends over the network

    

      On your first click of "Extract", this page injects the
      @paddlejs-models/ocr script (from jsDelivr) and lets it fetch
      its detection/recognition model weight files (from Baidu's model CDN,
      *.bcebos.com) — those are one-way downloads of ~a few MB of
      model weights, the same every time, containing no data from you. After
      that one-time load, every image you upload and every OCR result stays on
      this device: ocr.recognize() runs entirely via WebGL in your
      browser, and nothing in this page's code makes a network request that
      carries the image, the raw OCR text, or the parsed fields anywhere.
